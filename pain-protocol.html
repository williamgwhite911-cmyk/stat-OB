Trauma Star Pain Protocol

One-Page Pain Protocol

Adult · OB Non-Labor · OB Labor · Peds — Fentanyl → Ketamine → Hydromorphone
DIFFERENTIATE FIRST. Broken-bone / fracture / soft-tissue / non-obstetric pain → use Adult, Peds, or OB Non-Labor column. Labor pain (contractions + cervical change) → OB Labor column (neuraxial first). Pregnant patient at any GA: ketamine is contraindicated for analgesia. NSAIDs: avoid > 20 wk, strictly avoid > 28 wk.
Adult non-pregnant · acute pain
1

Fentanyl

50 mcg/mL vial · 1st-line opioid
When
Moderate-severe acute pain
Dose
0.5 – 1 mcg/kg IV · repeat q 5-10 min PRN · MAX cumulative 200 mcg
Reassess
5 min — pain score, RR, SpO2, BP, sedation
Document
Dose · time · route · pre & post pain score · RR · SpO2
2

Ketamine — Analgesic

500 mg / 10 mL (50 mg/mL) · sub-dissociative · opioid-sparing
When
Pain refractory to fentanyl OR opioid-sparing desired (poly-trauma, opioid-tolerant)
Dose
Mild / elderly >75 / fragile: 0.1 – 0.3 mg/kg IV over 1-2 min
Mod-severe stable: 0.3 – 0.5 mg/kg IV over 1-2 min
Reassess
5 min — repeat only if needed; watch for partial dissociation
Document
Dose · time · pre & post pain · any dissociative effects · BP · HR
3

Hydromorphone (Dilaudid)

2 mg/mL or 4 mg/mL vial · longer-acting opioid
When
Persistent pain after Steps 1+2 OR long transport requiring durable analgesia
Dose
Most adults: 0.2 – 0.8 mg IV over 2-3 min
Opioid-tolerant: 0.5 – 1.5 mg IV
Repeat q 30-60 min PRN, titrated
Reassess
15-30 min — sedation, RR, pain score
Document
Dose · time · sedation score · RR · SpO2 · response
OB Non-Labor pregnant · trauma / fracture / non-obstetric
1

Fentanyl

50 mcg/mL vial · 1st-line opioid in pregnancy
When
Acute pain (fracture, soft-tissue, non-labor)
Dose
0.5 – 1 mcg/kg IV · repeat q 5-10 min PRN · MAX cumulative 200 mcg
Reassess
5 min — maternal RR/SpO2/BP, FHR if monitored
Document
Dose · time · GA (weeks) · pre & post pain · FHR · L-lateral tilt >20 wk
2

Ketamine — CONTRAINDICATED

do not use for analgesia in pregnancy
Why
Catecholamine surge → ↑ uterine tone, ↓ uteroplacental perfusion. Skip directly to Step 3.
3

Hydromorphone (Dilaudid)

2 mg/mL or 4 mg/mL vial · longer-acting opioid
When
Persistent pain after fentanyl OR long transport
Dose
0.2 – 0.8 mg IV over 2-3 min · repeat q 30-60 min PRN, titrated. Lowest effective dose.
Reassess
15-30 min — maternal sedation/RR, FHR
Document
Dose · time · GA · FHR before & after · neonatal-naloxone availability if near delivery
⚠
NSAIDs: avoid > 20 wk; strictly avoid > 28 wk. L-lateral tilt > 20 wk.
OB Labor active labor · contractions + cervical change
1st

Neuraxial (preferred)

epidural / spinal · anesthesia + OB
When
Available at receiving facility — coordinate during transport
Action
Pre-notify; ensure IV access, baseline VS + FHR; L-lateral tilt > 20 wk
Document
Pre-notification time · maternal VS · FHR baseline
alt

Fentanyl

if neuraxial unavailable / not desired
Dose
50 – 100 mcg IV · repeat q 30-60 min PRN
Reassess
Maternal RR & sedation; FHR before and after every dose
Document
Dose · time · FHR pre/post · cervical exam if known · stage of labor
alt

Hydromorphone

adjunct / alternative opioid for labor
Dose
0.5 – 1 mg IV over 2-3 min · repeat q 2-3 hr PRN
Reassess
Maternal RR/sedation; FHR; signs of imminent delivery
Document
Dose · time · FHR pre/post · est time to delivery
⚠
Avoid near planned delivery — neonatal respiratory depression. Neonatal naloxone 0.1 mg/kg at hand.
✗

NO ketamine for labor

Why
Uterine hypertonus + altered maternal mentation contraindicated in labor.
Peds weight-based · max single per step
1

Fentanyl

50 mcg/mL vial · 1st-line opioid (IV/IN)
When
Acute pain, peds > neonatal
Dose
1 – 2 mcg/kg IV/IN · repeat q 5-10 min PRN · MAX single dose 100 mcg
Reassess
5 min — RR, SpO2, behavioral pain scale
Document
Wt (kg) · dose · time · route · pre & post pain · RR · SpO2
2

Ketamine — Analgesic

500 mg / 10 mL · sub-dissociative · IV or IN
When
Refractory to fentanyl OR opioid-sparing desired
Dose
Young / fragile: 0.1 – 0.3 mg/kg IV over 1-2 min
Mod-severe stable: 0.3 – 0.5 mg/kg IV over 1-2 min
IN route: 1 – 1.5 mg/kg IN (max 1 mL per nostril)
Reassess
5 min — pain, dissociation, HR/BP
Document
Wt · dose · route · time · pre & post pain · dissociative effects
3

Hydromorphone (Dilaudid)

2 mg/mL or 4 mg/mL vial · longer-acting
When
Persistent pain after Steps 1+2 OR long transport
Dose
0.01 – 0.02 mg/kg IV over 2-3 min · typical 0.05 – 0.2 mg per dose · repeat q 30-60 min PRN
Reassess
15-30 min — sedation, RR, pain
Document
Wt · dose · time · sedation score · RR · SpO2 · response

Reassess every dose

  • Pain score (NRS 0-10 adult; FLACC / Wong-Baker peds; verbal in labor)
  • Respiratory rate & SpO2 — adult < 10 / peds age-specific = hold next dose, consider naloxone
  • Sedation level (RASS or Pasero scale)
  • BP & HR — opioid hypotension in volume-depleted / hemorrhagic patient
  • OB only: FHR before AND after each opioid dose; signs of imminent delivery

Document at every administration

  • Medication, dose (with units), route, time
  • Patient weight (kg) used for the calculation
  • Pre- and post- pain score with timestamp
  • RR, SpO2, BP, sedation level pre & post
  • OB: gestational age, FHR pre/post, L-lateral tilt > 20 wk
  • Adverse effects (n/v, pruritus, dissociation, hypotension)
  • Naloxone availability at bedside (esp. peds & OB near delivery)
